Jiazheng Zhu · Astronomer

 Research Profile

I am a Ph.D. student with broad interests in time-domain astrophysics and optical transients. My research combines wide-field transient surveys with follow-up observations and multi-wavelength data analysis, with particular expertise in tidal disruption events (TDEs). I have experience across different stages of transient research — from survey observations and transient discovery to photometric and spectroscopic follow-up and physical interpretation.

Looking toward the era of increasingly deep time-domain surveys (particularly the Vera C. Rubin Observatory's Legacy Survey of Space and Time, LSST), I am especially interested in exploring the faint and still poorly sampled regimes of the transient sky. My future research will focus on using TDEs to probe black holes across new mass and redshift regimes — in particular TDEs from intermediate-mass black holes (IMBHs) and high-redshift TDEs.

 Research Interests

  • Time-domain Astrophysics & Optical Transients: discovery and physical characterization of optical transients using wide-field surveys, photometric and spectroscopic follow-up, and multi-wavelength observations.
  • Tidal Disruption Events: observational diversity, transient accretion physics, host-galaxy environments, and the use of TDEs as probes of otherwise quiescent black holes.
  • High-redshift TDEs: extending TDE discovery and spectroscopy toward higher redshift to study transient black-hole accretion and TDE populations across cosmic time.
  • Intermediate-mass Black Holes: searching for TDEs and other transient signatures of IMBHs in low-mass galaxies and other environments where dormant black holes are difficult to identify.
  • Faint & Unexplored Transient Populations: using increasingly deep time-domain surveys to uncover transient populations poorly sampled by previous surveys, including faint nuclear transients and unusual accretion events.
